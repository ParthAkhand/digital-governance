The problem it solves

Every city runs on reports that nobody independent checks.

01

Reports and the ground differ.

Contractors and zones report their own work. No independent team verifies it daily.

02

Decisions run on claims.

Bills are passed and staff are assessed on self-reported figures, so a review becomes one opinion against another.

03

The gap costs marks.

The Swachh Survekshan toolkit deducts marks when a city's portal claims do not match the field assessment. PRAGATI-50 cities are tracked on 48 indicators.

Why it has to be independent

A verified picture can only come from a team with no stake in it.

  • The team that wrote the plan cannot certify how it was carried out.
  • A dashboard shows what contractors and departments enter. It does not check it.
  • An agency working in a ward cannot inspect that ward.

reports only to the Commissioner and runs none of the services it checks. It comes as one contract with one accountable team, and the first verified review is due on day 30.

What we need from the city

The 30 days start when the first three items are in hand.

  1. A work order, and a letter from the Commissioner naming the PMU as the monitoring unit with access to contractor and zone data.
  2. Copies of every SWM contract in force, and the duty rosters for the city's own staff and vehicles.
  3. Ward, beat, route, vehicle and asset data, in the format we send.
  4. A written direction to each contractor and zone to report on the dashboard within 15 days.
  5. A nodal officer, seating and internet for the team, and a fixed weekly review slot with the Commissioner.

What the PMU does not do

  • It does not run any service, hire or pay field staff, or pass bills.
  • It does not supply hardware such as cameras, GPS devices or video walls.
  • It does not check its own group's work. Where holds any other contract in the city, that contract is reviewed by the city's officers, not by the PMU.
  • It does not design a project and then bid for it.
